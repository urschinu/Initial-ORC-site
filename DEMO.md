# Demo Walkthrough

After you run the site locally, check these screens first:

1. **Home**  
   Review hero, trust marker, research verticals, featured publications, events, notices, archive highlight, and partner strip.

2. **Research**  
   Open a vertical page such as `/research/kalinga-space-power-memory` to see linked publications and events driven by shared taxonomy.

3. **Publications**  
   Open a detail page such as `/publications/littoral-governance-odisha-policy-frictions` and test the placeholder PDF download.

4. **Governance**  
   Review the institutional policy/report listing to confirm the high-trust administrative section.

5. **Contact**  
   Verify directory layout, address block, and collaboration prompt.

## Intended demo impression

The site should feel:
- scholarly
- institutional
- archival
- editorially serious
- calm rather than flashy
